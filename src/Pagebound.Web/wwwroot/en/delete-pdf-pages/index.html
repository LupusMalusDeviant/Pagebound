Delete PDF pages

Remove pages from a PDF, put them in a new order or rotate them – and save the result as a new PDF. Everything happens in your browser.

Open page organizer Free, no sign-up. The first visit downloads a few MB once; after that it also works offline.

  • No uploadYour file is processed in the browser and never leaves your device.
  • No account, no costNo sign-up, no subscription, no watermarks. Open source (Apache 2.0).
  • No trackingNo telemetry, no ads, no third-party fonts or scripts.

How it works

  1. Open “Organize pages” and choose your PDF.
  2. Delete the pages you don’t need; drag to reorder or rotate if needed.
  3. “Apply & save” – the new PDF is downloaded. Your original stays untouched.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Pagebound runs entirely in the browser; the file is never sent to a server.

Can I undo a deletion?

Yes, as long as you haven’t saved yet (“Restore”). Your original is never overwritten anyway.

Is there a size limit?

There is no quota. Very large files (hundreds of pages) need correspondingly more memory in the browser.
